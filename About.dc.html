I spent five years in marketing before I found product design, and that background still shapes how I work. I spent enough time thinking about audiences, conversion, and what makes someone stick around. When I moved into design, it felt less like starting over and more like finally building the thing I'd been writing and strategizing around all along.

Different industries, same instinct: start with what's actually confusing someone, and design my way out from there.

The last year and a half has been full product redesigns across femtech, sports, and SaaS — rethinking core flows, rebuilding how people interact with an AI assistant, designing community and monetisation features for a fan platform, and co-founding a product from the ground up.

I like problems that don't have an obvious shape yet. The kind where you have to sit with the mess before anything clean comes out of it. Outside of work, that same instinct shows up in smaller ways: trying out recipes, making things with my hands, and losing an evening to a project just because I want to see it finished.

I'm currently open to new opportunities. If you think I'd be a good fit for your team, I'd love to hear from you.
