Case Study — Product Design · Co-Founder

Trends are everywhere. Making them make sense for you is the hard part.

JumpWag surfaces what's trending across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts — then helps you figure out how it actually fits your niche, audience, and brand.

The Idea

Finding a trend isn't the hard part anymore — everyone can see what's trending. The real question is what to actually do with it. JumpWag was built to answer that: not just "here's a trend," but "here's how to make it yours."

I was one of three co-founders, alongside a growth-focused PM and a developer. My role was to handle all of JumpWeg's product design, working closely with the other two on direction and scope.

The Core Flow — Idea Generation

The most important flow in the product: paste a trend link, and the AI analyzes it. From there, the user customizes how the idea should be shaped (format, like POV, on-screen text, or full script, plus target audience and CTA) before generating and refining it with built-in feedback.
